# Project instructions

- **Read [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) before any visual or content change.** It holds the
  design system, the decided conventions (breadcrumbs, product marks, footer, images),
  the accessibility floor and the definition of done.
- Before every `git push`, add a dated entry to [NOTES.md](NOTES.md) summarising what
  changed in the commits being pushed, then include that NOTES.md update in the same
  push. Keep entries short and specific (what changed, not why it matters).
